Policies, Terms & Service Level Agreements

Our commitment to transparency, data sovereignty, and professional engineering standards across all Iskra Exchange LLC architectures.

Section 01

Terms & Conditions

1. Consultation-First Framework

Iskra Exchange LLC operates as a bespoke B2B custom software architecture atelier. Initial inquiries, discovery sessions, and scoping briefs are diagnostic and advisory in nature, establishing mutual institutional alignment before any custom engineering agreement or subscription tier is executed.

2. Intellectual Property & Code Sovereignty

Custom software schemas, application source code, and tailored database structures engineered specifically for the Client remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Client upon full fulfillment of agreed financial retainer terms. Iskra Exchange LLC retains sole ownership of underlying proprietary core libraries, deployment toolkits, and foundational framework modules.

3. Data Privacy & Security

All client environments adhere to rigorous data protection standards, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 hardware encryption at rest. Client operational data is treated with strict confidentiality and is never brokered, mined, or disclosed to unauthorized third parties.

Section 02

Refund & Cancellation Policy

1. Retainer & Engineering Allocation

Because monthly retainers (**Monolith**, **Strata**, or **Apex**) immediately allocate dedicated principal engineering hours, continuous delivery sprint velocity, and cloud infrastructure provisioning upon the start of each billing cycle, monthly retainer fees are non-refundable once the billing period has commenced.

2. Agreement Termination & Offboarding

Either party may terminate a recurring monthly retainer by providing written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the subsequent billing cycle. Upon formal termination and settlement of any outstanding balances, Iskra Exchange LLC will securely package and transfer all application source code and client data archives back to the Client, followed by secure decommissioning of staging servers.
